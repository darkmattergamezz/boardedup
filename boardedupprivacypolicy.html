Boarded Up Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026 · Applies to the Boarded Up Android game (com.boardedup.game)

In short: Boarded Up does not ask you to create an account, does not show ads, and does not collect your name, email, or location. Your progress stays on your phone. The only thing that touches an outside service is the optional one-time purchase, which Google Play handles.

Who we are

Boarded Up is made by DarkMatterGamezz. Questions about this policy can be sent to darkmattergamezz@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (your survivor, the world, items you stored, journal entries, settings such as graphics quality and screen orientation, and best-run records) in the game's own storage on your phone. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the game or choose "Erase everything" in the settings, it is deleted.

What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, or profile.
  • No name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier use by the game itself.
  • No analytics or tracking code written into the game.

The full-game purchase

The free demo can be upgraded to the full game with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is processed by Google Play. Google receives your payment details under its own privacy policy, and we never see your card number or other payment information. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was completed, so that it can unlock the full game and restore it if you reinstall.

Third-party software

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Google Play Billing for purchases. These components run on your device and may exchange technical information with their providers as described in their own policies: Unity and Google.

Children

Boarded Up contains zombie violence, blood, and a dark tone. It is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Your choices

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on our side. You can remove everything the game stores on your phone by choosing "Erase everything" in the game's settings or by uninstalling the game. Purchases can be managed or refunded through Google Play.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding ads or online features, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.
